# Emergency Content Review

Emergency guidance changes require careful review.

- Identify the source used to author or update the guidance.
- Review the sequence and clarity of steps.
- Check warnings and escalation language.
- Verify translations with fluent reviewers.
- Test the rendered content online and offline.
- Keep a record of the content version and review date.

CrisisNav should direct users to official emergency services and must not replace professional instructions.
